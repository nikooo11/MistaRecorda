# MistaRecorda studio backgrounds

`studio-blueprint`, `studio-contour`, `studio-graphite`, and `studio-paper` are original procedural artwork created for this repository. They contain no stock photography, external fonts, logos, or third-party video. The PNGs and silent looping MP4s may be used in recordings and exported videos without attribution, including commercial use. To the extent copyright applies, these six assets are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/.

Recreate them with `scripts/generate-studio-backgrounds.py` using Pillow and the project's ffmpeg-static executable. Motion variants have an eight-second closed path at 1920×1080, 30 fps, H.264/yuv420p, with no audio track.
